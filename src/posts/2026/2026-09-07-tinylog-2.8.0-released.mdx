---
title: tinylog 2.8.0 released
---

After two milestones, finally the final version of tinylog 2.8.0 is there! Since no bugs were reported for the second milestone, it can be released unchanged as the final version 2.8.0. The new features can be found in the news posts of the [first](/2024/04/first-milestone-of-tinylog-2.8-is-out/) and [second](/2026/08/second-milestone-of-tinylog-2.8-is-out/) milestone.
